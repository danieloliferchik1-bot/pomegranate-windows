Privacy Policy

Effective November 19, 2026

Pomegranate Windows (“we,” “us”) is a window and door installation company licensed in California (CA Lic. #1065271). This page explains what information our website collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it simple: we only collect what we need to give you a quote and do the work.

Information you give us

When you request a free quote on our website, you tell us:

  • What you want to replace and roughly how many windows or doors
  • When you’re hoping to start
  • Your ZIP code and whether you own the home
  • Your name, phone number and, if you choose, your email address

We also keep the details you share with us when you call, text or email us.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, ours collects basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser and device type, and the pages you view. This comes from our website host, Netlify, and from the Meta Pixel described below.

How we use your information

  • To call or message you about your quote and schedule your free in-home estimate
  • To prepare your quote and complete your project
  • To answer your questions and provide warranty service
  • To understand how people find our website and improve our advertising

Meta Pixel and cookies

We use the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta (the company behind Facebook and Instagram), to measure how well our ads work, for example how many people request a quote after seeing one. Meta may use cookies and similar technology to collect information about your visit and may use it as described in Meta’s Privacy Policy. You can change how Meta uses this information for ads in your Facebook ad preferences, and you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

How we share information

We don’t sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that help us run our business, such as Netlify, which hosts our website and receives quote requests, and the notification service that sends new requests to our phone
  • With Meta, through the Meta Pixel, as described above
  • When required by law, or to protect our rights and the safety of others

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it to respond to you, complete and support your project, and keep normal business and tax records.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you’ve given us, or ask us to stop contacting you, at any time. Just email info@pomegranatewindows.com or call (916) 848-8440.

Do Not Track

Some browsers can send a “Do Not Track” signal. Our website does not currently change how it works in response to these signals.

Children

Our website is meant for homeowners and is not directed to children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we’ll post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.

Questions? Contact Pomegranate Windows at info@pomegranatewindows.com or (916) 848-8440.
