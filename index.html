FAQ

Questions before you get a quote?

How much does window replacement cost?

Price depends on size, frame material and glass package. We give you a clear, written quote at a free in-home estimate, with no pressure to sign that day.

How long does installation take?

A typical home of 8 to 12 windows is finished in one or two days. Patio and entry doors usually take half a day each.

Will new windows lower my energy bill?

Yes. Double-pane, low-E, argon-filled windows cut drafts and heat transfer compared with old single-pane or failed-seal units.

Do you offer a warranty?

Every install includes a manufacturer warranty on the product and our own workmanship warranty on the installation.

Can you replace just one window or door?

Yes. We handle single-window jobs, a whole house, or anything in between.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. The in-home visit and written quote cost nothing, and you're under no obligation to buy.
